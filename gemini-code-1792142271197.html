3. FREE INSTALLATION GUIDE FOR USERS

  1. Click both download buttons above to get manifest.json and content.js.
  2. Put both files together in a new folder on your computer named RepulsCrosshair.
  3. Open Google Chrome / Brave / Edge and go to chrome://extensions.
  4. Turn on Developer mode (toggle in top right).
  5. Click Load unpacked (top left) and select your RepulsCrosshair folder.
  6. Open repuls.io to play with your custom crosshair!
