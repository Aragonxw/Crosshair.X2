CROSHAIRx² Guide

Features and quick start instructions for Repuls.io custom reticles:

  • 24x24 Pixel Designer: Draw pixel-perfect reticles with paint, line, and preset tools.
  • Presets & Funny Shapes: Includes Heart, Smiley, Skull, Biohazard, Diamond Star, Donut Ring, and Scope presets.
  • Dynamic Animations: Choose 360° Spin, Pulse, Rainbow Spectrum Cycle, and Glitch Radar Sweep effects.
  • Interactive 3D Dummy: Click the target dummy mannequin in the 3D viewport to toggle armor color themes!
  • Tampermonkey Script Generator: Automated Tampermonkey userscripts with verified multi-stage pointer-lock screenlock detection for live gameplay.
